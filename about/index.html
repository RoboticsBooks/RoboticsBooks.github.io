About Indus Programming

Practical robot programming, from someone who commissions robot cells.

I'm Guillaume Olivier, an automation engineer. I have spent years programming and commissioning industrial robot cells, mainly FANUC and ABB, in several companies.

Training courses teach the basics well, but they rarely cover what you face during a real project: configuring the fieldbus, loading a program onto a brand-new robot, designing reliable handshakes with the PLC, or structuring a program that keeps running in production. I created Indus Programming to share the methods that proved reliable on the shop floor.

The books

  • FANUC Robot Programming: TP programming built step by step in Roboguide.
  • ABB Robot Programming: RAPID with RobotStudio and the FlexPendant. Also available in French.
