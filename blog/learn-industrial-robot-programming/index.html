3 Ways to Learn Industrial Robot Programming

Training courses, self-learning or learning from real projects: the pros and cons of each way to learn ABB and FANUC robot programming.

New to programming robots? Not sure where to begin? Here are 3 ways to get started or improve your skills!

Attend a Training Program

One of the best ways to learn industrial robot programming is by enrolling in a training program.

Manufacturers and independent centers offer specialized courses that typically span a week, catering to various skill levels ranging from basics to advanced programming and even specific areas like welding and vision applications. If you're a service technician, there are dedicated courses tailored to your needs as well.

These training programs provide valuable hands-on experience and cover the fundamentals of robot programming. You'll gain insights into the essentials of programming and learn how to manipulate robots. However, from my experiences, I've noticed some critical aspects missing from such courses:

  • How to configure the fieldbus?

  • How to load a program on a new robot?

  • What are the steps for commissioning a new robot?

  • How to optimize a program to reduce the cycle time?

  • How to build an effective, reliable, multi-reference program?

Learn on Your Own

Another way to learn industrial robot programming is self-learning.

Whether you choose to start with a real robot example or build from scratch, you can rely on a wealth of resources such as documentation, manuals, online forums, Reddit communities, and YouTube tutorials.

Here are some useful resources:

  • Youtube: Official channel ABB RobotStudio Tutorials 

  • Forum: Robot-Forum.com

  • Reddit: r/plc

Immerse yourself in a learning process of experimenting with various solutions through trial and error. Embrace mistakes as an essential part of learning, but be aware that with robots, consequences can be more impactful.

Learn from Experienced Professionals and Real-Life Examples

Learning from experienced individuals in the field is a valuable way to accelerate your progress. This approach enables you to avoid common pitfalls and sidestep the need to start from scratch or rely on not so good example programs.

If you prefer self-paced learning, consider reading my books on ABB and FANUC robot programming. This guide covers everything from the basics to advanced techniques, offering step-by-step instructions, real-world examples, and practical tips to help you master robot programming. With this ebook, you'll have the flexibility of self-paced learning and can revisit the material whenever needed.

On this site you will also find free tutorials and tools to help you get started.

In conclusion, whether you choose to attend a training program, embrace trial and error, or learn from real-world examples, the key is to practice and persevere. With dedication and the right resources, you can become proficient in industrial robot programming.
